About Neogi Para Nagarikbrinda

Fostering unity, socio-cultural harmony, and community development in Baranagar.

Our History & Legacy

Established with a vision to unite the residents of Neogi Para Road and surrounding areas, Neogi Para Nagarikbrinda has grown into a cornerstone of civic, cultural, and socio-welfare activity in Baranagar, Kolkata. Through decades of dedicated effort, our organization has continuously brought families together across generations through festival celebrations, community aid, and youth engagement.

Our Core Objectives

Cultural Heritage

Organizing grand festival celebrations like Sharodotsav, Shyama Puja, and Dol Utsav to keep traditional Bengali art, music, and culture alive.

Community Welfare

Conducting health check-up camps, blood donation drives, educational support programs, and emergency assistance for neighborhood residents.

Youth & Talent Development

Hosting annual sit-and-draw contests, sports competitions, and workshops to nurture creative and athletic talent among the local youth.
