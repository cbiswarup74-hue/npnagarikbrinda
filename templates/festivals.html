ॐ Sharodotsav Notes Grand Event

Our flagship socio-cultural celebration brings together residents across all generations with warmth, devotion, and festive joy.

  • Chaturthi Cultural Evening: Inaugurated with vibrant musical, dance, and drama performances by neighborhood residents.
  • Bijoya Dashami & Baran Utsav: Traditional Sindoor Khela, auspicious Debi Baran rituals, and heartfelt exchange of Bijoya greetings.
  • Community Feast: A special get-together organized a few days after Dashami, where all members gather to enjoy delicious bhog and meals together.

🪔 Shyama Puja & Diwali Autumn Illuminations

The festival of lights was celebrated with traditional grandeur and luminous enthusiasm across Neogi Para.

  • Diwali & Midnight Puja: Conducted sacred midnight Shyama Puja worship amidst solemn chants and diya illuminations.
  • Fire Crackers & Celebrations: The neighborhood lit up with colorful fireworks, sparkles, and oil lamps, sharing sweets and joy with every household.

🌸 Dol Utsav & Basanta Utsav Spring Festival

A colorful celebration welcoming spring with melody, laughter, and community fellowship.

  • Splash & Splatter of Colors: Celebrated with vibrant dry abir in shades of red, yellow, pink, and blue exchanged among all members.
  • Songs & Rabindra Sangeet: Morning Prabhat Pheri recitals and chorus songs celebrating spring.
  • Sweets & Shared Joy: Distribution of traditional sweets, snacks, and warm festive wishes across the community.
